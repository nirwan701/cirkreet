# Code of Conduct

CirKreet aims to be a welcoming, respectful learning and engineering space.
All contributors and participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive when discussing technical differences.
- Welcome questions from people with different levels of experience.
- Give feedback that focuses on the work rather than the person.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and learn from feedback.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and disruptive behaviour.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict
participation when behaviour is abusive or repeatedly violates these
expectations. For concerns, contact the repository maintainers through GitHub.
